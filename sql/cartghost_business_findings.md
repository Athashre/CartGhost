# CartGhost — E-commerce Order Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

CartGhost is an e-commerce data analytics portfolio project that uses Python, pandas, and PostgreSQL to examine order performance, customer activity, monthly trends, and potential customer churn.

**Dataset:** Synthetic e-commerce order data  
**Analysis period:** January 1 – October 22, 2026  
**Tools:** Python, pandas, PostgreSQL, SQL, Matplotlib

## 2. Business Questions

- How many orders were completed, cancelled, or returned?
- What was the total value of completed orders?
- How did completed order volume and order value change month over month?
- Which product categories generated the highest completed order value?
- Which customers meet the project's rule-based potential churn definition?

## 4. Methodology

Only completed orders are used for completed-order value, average order value, and monthly performance analysis.

Potential churn is defined as at least two completed orders and at least 45 days since the customer's most recent completed order, measured on October 22, 2026. This is a rule-based segment, not a machine-learning prediction.

## 5. Data Limitations

The dataset is synthetic and does not represent real customers or a real business. Findings demonstrate the analytical workflow and should not be presented as evidence of actual market behavior.
